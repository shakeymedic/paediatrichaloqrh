## Full description (4,000 characters max)

```
Paediatric HALO QRH is a quick reference handbook for High Acuity, Low Occurrence emergency procedures in children and young people under 16. It is written for trained doctors, nurses and paramedics who may need to perform a rare, time-critical procedure on a child and want a clear checklist to follow.

Each procedure has two parts:
• Prepare: the walkthrough to read while the team gets ready, with paediatric doses, sizes and tips.
• Action: the steps to follow while doing it, with labelled diagrams for most procedures.

Procedures in this version (v1.3):
• Resuscitative hysterotomy
• Emergency breech delivery
• Resuscitative thoracotomy
• Escharotomy (burns)
• Open chest drain
• Pericardiocentesis
• External pacing
• Subclavian / brachiocephalic central line
• Lateral canthotomy and cantholysis
• Severe maxillofacial bleed
• LAST (local anaesthetic systemic toxicity, lipid rescue)
• Surgical airway (eFONA and surgical tracheotomy)
• DC cardioversion
Plus a WETFLAG reference card and the STOP 5 hot debrief.

What the app does:
• Works with no signal: everything is stored on the phone.
• Search the procedures by name, drug or kit.
• Tick off steps as you go.
• Download the full printable PDF handbook to your phone.
• Keeps the screen on while the app is open.

Who it is for: trained healthcare professionals caring for patients under 16. For adults, use HALO QRH (adults).

Important: Paediatric HALO QRH is a cognitive aid and teaching resource. It does not replace training, local policy or clinical judgement, and guidelines change. It is not a medical device. The content draws on published UK guidance (for example Resuscitation Council UK, DAS and RCOG), but those organisations have not produced or endorsed this app.

The app has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking, and it collects no personal data.

Published by West Midlands Evidence Based Emergency Medicine Ltd (EM Evidence).
```

## Graphics (Play's rules)

- **App icon:** 512 × 512 px, 32-bit PNG, up to 1 MB: `icons/icon-512.png` (purple, "PQRH", so it is not
  mistaken for the adult app).
- **Feature graphic:** 1024 × 500 px, JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no transparency.
- **Phone screenshots:** at least 2 (4 or more at 1080 × 1920 is recommended), JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no device
  frames, no "Best", "#1", prices or "Download now", and no text covering more than a fifth of the picture.
- Ready-made files: `/mnt/project-files/plans/play-store/paeds-halo-qrh/` in the EM Evidence project.

## App content answers

The same as the adult HALO QRH (see [README.md](README.md#play-console-answers)), with these points to watch:

- **Target audience:** 18 and over only. The app is about children but is for clinicians; ticking an under-18
  age group would bring in Play's Families policy.
- **Content rating:** the diagrams include a pregnant abdomen (hysterotomy, breech delivery) and surgical
  drawings, as in the adult app. Answer the questionnaire the same way as for the adult app.
- **Health apps declaration:** Medical reference and education; not a medical device.
